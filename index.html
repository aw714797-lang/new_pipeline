How a bag gets here

Every lot goes through the same four checkpoints before it's bagged — the only thing that changes is the bean.

01

Cup before we commit

We cup every offered lot blind against three prior harvests from the same farm before agreeing on a price.

02

Roast in small drums

12kg batches on a 1957 Probat, adjusted per lot — density and moisture content change the curve every time.

03

Rest before release

Bags rest a minimum of 5 days post-roast so CO2 can escape and the cup settles into its actual flavor.

04

Bag with the full record

Farm, altitude, process, and roast date go on every bag — no blend names, no marketing labels.
